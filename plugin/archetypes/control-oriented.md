# Control-oriented

**Funny alias**: "Can you send me a quick detailed breakdown?"

## Observable signals
- Asks for detailed breakdowns of what changed and why
- Wants to review decisions before they are executed
- Requests options and recommendations, not just outcomes
- Asks "why wasn't I informed?" when surprises occur
- Sends follow-up questions after receiving a summary
- Uses phrases like "just keeping visibility", "loop me in", "keep me posted"
- Comments on PRs with questions about rationale and alternatives

## Underlying need
Visibility, predictability, and a sense of control over outcomes — not because they distrust the team, but because they feel responsible for what happens and need to see the evidence.

## Communication strategy
- Detail level: **high**
- Update frequency: **weekly minimum, daily if anything changes**
- Format preference: structured report with numbered sections, decisions documented, and explicit options
- Tone: calm, explicit, non-defensive

## Avoid
- Surprise decisions — surface every significant choice before executing
- Short vague updates ("everything is fine")
- Completing something and mentioning it casually after the fact
- Ambiguous next steps
- Emotional or defensive language when questioned

## Best output format
`useless-report:generate-control-report`

Report sections: Summary → Completed → Decisions made → Decision rationale → Options → Recommendation → Risks → Asks → Traceability appendix

## Example trigger messages
- "Can you send me a detailed breakdown of what changed this week?"
- "Why was this decision made without alignment?"
- "Please keep me updated before merging anything significant."
- "Can I see the options before we choose a direction?"
- "I'd like to review this before it goes live."
